The Words Students See

The AI use labels

  • No AI. You do this work on your own. Your instructor chose this label because the skill itself is what you're practicing.
  • AI-supported. You can use AI for the parts your instructor names, like brainstorming or checking grammar. The thinking and the final work are yours.
  • AI-integrated. Using AI well is part of the assignment. You might check what AI gives you against a standard, or explain how you used AI.

Students are told to find the label at the top of the assignment directions, and to ask you before they use AI if an assignment doesn't have one.

The 3 Qs

The 3 Qs and the BE behaviors behind them
MomentThe questionBehind it
Before you ask AIShould I use AI for this part of my assignment?BE Responsible, BE Honest
Before you use AI's answerHow would I know if AI's answer is wrong?BE Critical
Before you turn in your workCan I explain my work and how AI helped me?BE Honest, BE Reflective

BE Safe applies every time: never paste other people's personal information, passwords, or anything private into an AI tool. Students see the behaviors on the overview page. The detail pages stick to the questions.

Keep a trail

  • Write in Word, saved to OneDrive. Word keeps a dated history of the draft.
  • Keep a OneNote notebook for each course. Notes, sources, and ideas, dated as they go.
  • Write down how you use AI. The question, the part of the answer they used, and what they kept, changed, or left out.

The reason students are given: tools that try to spot AI writing are sometimes wrong, and a trail is real evidence of their own work.

Two more lines students see everywhere

  • When AI is optional, choosing not to use it is a good choice too.
  • Use Microsoft Copilot, signed in with your college account, unless your instructor names a different tool.
Keep the Words the Same

When you write your own student-facing text, such as your AI Course Policy, an assignment label, or feedback, use these terms word for word:

UseInstead of
AI use label (No AI, AI-supported, AI-integrated)AI level, AI tier, AI permission. "Level" belongs to the OCTC framework's Levels 1–3.
